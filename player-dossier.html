The objective

Eliminate your target. Survive.

Someone else is hunting you at the same time. Every player is both hunter and hunted — one closed loop, no exceptions.

Winner priority
1
The last player standing wins.
2
If multiple players are alive at game end, the highest kill count wins.
3
If tied on kill count, whoever landed their final kill first wins.

The kill chain

Mechanic

Every player sits inside one continuous loop. When you eliminate your target, you inherit their target. The chain closes behind you. The hunt continues.

This repeats until one player remains, or the winner-priority rules apply.

Example: Aditi hunts Rohan, who hunts Kabir. The moment Aditi eliminates Rohan, she inherits his target — she now hunts Kabir directly. Kabir never knew Rohan was in danger. Nobody ever receives their own name. The loop always closes.

Confirming a kill

Killer & victim procedure

Killer — generate a 6-digit kill code and show it to your target in person.

Victim — enter the code you were shown to confirm your own elimination.

Codes expire 10 minutes after generation — regenerate as many times as needed. Only your named victim can confirm it, and the system rejects any invalid code. Only generate a code when you're actually about to make the kill. Dead players cannot generate codes.
Rules of engagement — binding
1
The game is always active — it never pauses unless a rule below exempts it.
2
3rd Floor exemption: a target seated at their desk, chair, or floor is protected. Wait until they stand. Kills are never allowed in the 3rd floor breakout room or washrooms.
3
Witnesses are fine — kills may be seen by other active players or non-participants.
4
Contact required: physical contact plus saying "you're dead" (or equivalent) must happen before showing the code. Silent kills are void.
5
Work is protected: standing alone at your desk working is not protection, but you're safe during a meeting, call, or presentation until it ends.
6
No camping: avoiding the floor, other players, or slipping off home to dodge a hunt isn't fair play and may lead to disqualification at the moderator's discretion.
When in doubt, wait. A disputed kill helps no one. Contact the moderator only for a genuine dispute — once a victim enters the code, there's no appeal.

Alive players race to claim immunity on the portal — limited slots, fastest valid claims win. Submit once; spamming doesn't help. You're never told if you won a slot.

Fastest fingers first

The first N players to claim inside the window win a slot — N is fixed and announced beforehand. If N = 5, the first 5 fastest claims win; the 6th doesn't, even a second behind.

What happens in a kill
1
First valid attack while immune: the kill fails, you survive, immunity is consumed. Both killer and victim are notified automatically.
2
Any attack after that resolves normally — once immunity is used, you're vulnerable again.
Only alive players can claim, one claim per player. Miss the window and no claim is submitted for you.

Ghost protocol

Second chance — two-stage vote

Stage 1 — Alive vote: alive players vote for one dead or ghost player to revive.

Stage 2 — Dead vote: dead players vote which alive player the revived ghost must assassinate.

For the ghost vote, share your SID once voting opens — think of it as a mini campaign. Voting priority: highest vote count wins; ties go to whoever reached that tally first.
If you're revived
Revived
You re-enter the game as a Ghost with a specific target assigned by the dead players' vote.
Your mission
Eliminate your assigned target using the normal kill code system. They don't know they're being hunted.
Time limit
You have 2 hours. Kill in time and you're fully alive again, inheriting their hunter and their victim.
If you fail
Time runs out → permanent elimination. No further chances.
